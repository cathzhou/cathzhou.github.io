As a Research Assistant at the Ken Chen Lab in MD Anderson Cancer Center, I'm working on cutting-edge projects analyzing the tumor microenvironment in head and neck cancer using single-cell RNA sequencing approaches.

## Research Focus

Our work focuses on understanding the complex interactions within the tumor microenvironment, particularly:
- Analyzing scRNA-seq transcriptomic data from over 100 head and neck cancer biopsy samples
- Profiling fibroblast populations and their roles in cancer progression
- Developing CNN models with transfer learning to predict binding interactions

## Technical Approach

The project leverages advanced computational methods:

### Single-Cell Analysis Pipeline
- Preprocessing and quality control of scRNA-seq data
- Cell type identification and clustering
- Trajectory analysis and pseudotime ordering
- Differential expression analysis

### Machine Learning Implementation
- Convolutional Neural Networks for binding prediction
- Integration of multiple data types:
  - Expression data
  - Conservation metrics
  - Interaction profiles

## Recognition

This work has been recognized through:
- Selection as a CPRIT-CURE scholar
- Award of travel fellowship to present at the 2024 NIH/NCI ITCR Annual Conference

The findings from this research contribute to our understanding of cancer progression and potential therapeutic targets in head and neck cancer.
