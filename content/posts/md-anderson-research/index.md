As a Research Assistant at the Ken Chen Lab in MD Anderson Cancer Center, I'm working on cutting-edge projects analyzing the tumor microenvironment in head and neck cancer using advanced single-cell RNA sequencing approaches.

## Research Focus

My work focuses on understanding the complex interactions within the tumor microenvironment, particularly:
- Analyzing scRNA-seq transcriptomic data from clinical head and neck cancer biopsy samples
- Profiling and characterizing cancer fibroblast populations and their roles in cancer progression

## Technical Approach

The project leverages advanced computational methods:

### Single-Cell Analysis Pipeline
- Preprocessing and quality control of scRNA-seq data
- Cell type identification and clustering
- Trajectory analysis and pseudotime ordering
- Differential expression analysis
- Copy number variation and cell-cell interaction analysis

## Recognition

This work has been recognized through:
- Selection as a CPRIT-CURE scholar
- Award of travel fellowship to present at the 2024 NIH/NCI ITCR Annual Conference

The findings from this research contribute to our understanding of cancer progression and potential therapeutic targets in head and neck cancer.
