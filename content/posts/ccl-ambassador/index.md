> As an [Ambassador for the Center for Civic Leadership (CCL) at Rice University](https://ccl.rice.edu/mta), I work to connect students with transformative community engagement opportunities, particularly helping STEM students discover ways to apply their skills to social challenges. The CCL has been instrumental in nurturing my interest in volunteering and helping me bridge my academic pursuits with community engagement.

## About
My journey with the Center for Civic Leadership (CCL) began during my freshman year when I joined an Alternative Spring Break (ASB) program focused on health and homelessness. This experience opened my eyes to the power of community-driven solutions and how I could apply technical skills to address social issues. Since then, the CCL has been instrumental in nurturing my interest in volunteering and helping me bridge my academic pursuits with community engagement. I was awarded a Goliard Fellowship through the CCL to explore the intersection of religion and social issues in Thailand. Inspired by the challenges I witnessed there, particularly around sex trafficking, I lead an ASB program addressing this complex issue in Houston.

### Program Development
