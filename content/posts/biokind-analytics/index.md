> As Director of Biokind Analytics at Rice University, I lead client partnerships, chapter development, and data science initiatives, managing projects that analyze over $500M in combined nonprofit funds while mentoring student teams and expanding our organization's impact.

## Leadership Role & Impact

As one of the Directors at Biokind Analytics, I collaborate with the leadership team to drive our organization's mission of empowering healthcare nonprofits through data science. My role encompasses both strategic leadership and hands-on project management:

### Client Partnership Development
- Spearhead community outreach to identify and secure nonprofit partnerships
- Lead introductory client meetings to define project scope and objectives
- Oversee data acquisition and project setup processes
- Serve as the primary point of contact for partner organizations
- Manage project timelines from initial outreach to final evaluation

### Chapter Leadership & Growth
- Build and expand Biokind's presence at Rice University
- Plan and conduct general body meetings and recruitment initiatives
- Develop and run R and Python training seminars for team members
- Coordinate fundraising efforts to support organization growth
- Mentor team leads and ensure project quality and timely delivery

## Project Management Cycle

### Pre-Semester Planning (June-August)
- Lead client outreach and partnership development (1-2 month process)
- Conduct introductory meetings with potential partners
- Define project scopes and data requirements
- Establish project goals and success metrics

### Team Building & Development (September)
- Direct chapter recruitment initiatives
- Conduct team lead interviews and selection
- Form and structure project teams
- Organize technical training sessions and seminars

### Project Oversight (October-December)
- Monitor project progress and ensure alignment with goals
- Host regular team lead check-ins
- Provide technical and strategic guidance
- Maintain client communication and relationship management
- Begin outreach for upcoming semester projects

### Project Completion (December)
- Oversee final evaluations and deliverables
- Facilitate final presentations to nonprofit partners
- Gather feedback and identify areas for improvement
- Plan and organize upcoming semester initiatives

## Organization Impact

Since it was founded, Biokind Analytics has achieved significant growth:

- Expanded to 23 chapter institutions including Brown University and University of Virginia
- Completed 50+ projects analyzing over $500M in combined donations
- Developed comprehensive data analysis services:
  - Donor retention analysis and prediction
  - Patient demographic profiling
  - Event optimization and impact assessment
  - Resource allocation modeling

## Previous Experience

### Team Lead: Children at Risk (Houston, TX)

As Team Lead for the Children at Risk (C@R) project, I directed the data analysis and visualization efforts for their biennial "Growing Up in Houston" publication, a comprehensive report on children's quality of life in Harris County. This high-impact project serves as a crucial resource for grant writers, nonprofit leaders, and parents throughout the Houston area.

#### Project Scope & Impact
- Led visualization development for key metrics across multiple domains:
  - Demographics and population trends
  - Educational outcomes and early childhood education
  - Safety and vulnerability indicators
  - Overall child wellness metrics
- Designed accessible, cohesive visualizations for diverse stakeholders
- Created narrative-driven analysis for public consumption
- Supported grant-writing efforts of multiple organizations

#### Technical Approach
- Synthesized multiple complex datasets into clear, actionable insights
- Maintained consistent color coding and design patterns
- Developed concise trend analysis narratives
- Created event-based analysis (e.g., superintendent changes)
- Prioritized simplicity and digestibility for non-technical audiences
