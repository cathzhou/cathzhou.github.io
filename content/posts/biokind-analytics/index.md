As Team Lead and Analyst at Biokind Analytics at Rice, I've been working at the intersection of data science and healthcare, helping nonprofits make data-driven decisions to improve their community impact.

## Project Impact

Our team has made significant contributions to healthcare nonprofits through:
- Analysis of 20+ years of patient data from Candlelighters, a childhood cancer support organization
- Development of interactive visualizations and maps to identify service gaps
- Implementation of statistical models to optimize program growth and resource allocation

## Technical Approach

### Data Analysis Pipeline
- Cleaned and standardized historical data from multiple sources
- Developed automated reporting systems
- Created interactive dashboards for real-time monitoring

### Visualization and Modeling
- Interactive maps showing service coverage and needs
- Statistical models for program impact assessment
- Predictive analytics for resource allocation

## Key Outcomes

Our work has helped nonprofits:
- Identify underserved communities
- Optimize resource allocation
- Improve program effectiveness
- Make data-driven decisions for expansion

This experience has demonstrated the power of applying data analytics to solve real-world healthcare challenges and improve community services.
