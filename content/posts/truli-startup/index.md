> As the founder of Truli, I’m building an AI-powered platform that helps supplement and wellness brands create FDA-compliant labels, claims, and content—empowering small businesses to scale responsibly while navigating complex health regulations.

## About

Truli started from a simple insight: compliance shouldn't be a bottleneck for innovation in wellness. As a solo founder, I’ve architected the platform from the ground up—combining natural language processing, regulatory databases, and web integrations to streamline content creation for supplement companies.

### Core Product Features
- **FDA Regulation-Powered AI:** Built a structured index from 1000+ pages of 21 CFR Part 101 for real-time labeling checks  
- **Automated Content Generator:** Converts ingredients and health benefits into compliant supplement facts, claims, and disclaimers  
- **Database Integration:** Integrated FDA GRAS, ingredient safety, and nutrition databases into app for seamless product development  
- **Platform-Specific Optimization:** Generates outputs tailored for Amazon listings, DTC websites, and social media marketing  
- **Compliance Transparency:** Links every generated claim to the underlying FDA regulation for audit-ready transparency

## Vision & Impact

The supplement industry is booming—but many founders struggle to navigate compliance, leaving them vulnerable to FDA and FTC enforcement. Truli aims to make regulatory intelligence accessible, actionable, and scalable:

- Democratize regulatory understanding for small businesses  
- Prevent misleading or illegal claims before they’re published  
- Shorten product development cycles by 80% with AI-generated outputs  
- Create a safer, more transparent wellness market for consumers
